## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: The journal panel

Pressing J SHALL toggle a HUD panel listing every trial with its best tier medal and best
time, errand and vantage counts, glowseeds found and the next unlock, and campaign
progress, without releasing pointer lock. Opening it SHALL close the satchel and vice versa.

#### Scenario: Journal shows a gold with time

- **WHEN** Sun Courier has a gold clear at 1:12.4
- **THEN** the journal row shows a gold medal glyph and 1:12.4
