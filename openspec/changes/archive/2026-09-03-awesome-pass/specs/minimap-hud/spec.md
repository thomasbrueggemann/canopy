## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Glowseed dots and journal key

The minimap SHALL draw uncollected glowseeds within map range as faint pale-green dots, and
the intro controls table SHALL list the leaf-sail and the journal key.

#### Scenario: Seed dot within range

- **WHEN** an uncollected glowseed lies within the minimap radius
- **THEN** a 2 px pale-green dot marks it
